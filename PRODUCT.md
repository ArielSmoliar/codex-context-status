# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Codex users working through long tasks who want to inspect recorded context usage and decide when to continue, prepare a handoff, or start another chat.

## Product Purpose

Offer a compact inspector with capacity first, cache second, and expandable technical details. Explain the limits of recorded telemetry and make session transitions practical.

## Brand Personality

Clear, restrained, factual. Follow the host Codex experience rather than introducing a separate visual identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid a dashboard full of cards, decorative motion, and duplicate readings in chat prose that become stale after Refresh.

## Design Principles

- Keep readings and advice in the refreshable inspector.
- Separate observed evidence from estimates and user intent.
- Preserve the user's controls and expanded details during refresh.
- Offer explicit actions without silently messaging or creating chats.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the Codex theme, use keyboard-accessible native controls and readable labels, and keep motion minimal. State must be understandable without color.
